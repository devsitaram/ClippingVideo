# Privacy Policy — 2SRT (Clipper)

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

2SRT is a private tool that publishes videos, photos and text posts to Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that its
owner manages. It is not offered to the public.

## What data we use
- **Access tokens** for the Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts you connect, so the app can publish posts and read
  their view counts.
- **Page and account details** (name, id) needed to choose where a post goes.
- **Post statistics** (views, likes, comments) of posts the app published, to report how they perform.

## How it is stored
All data stays on the owner's own computer, in a local database. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Nothing is sold,
shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## How long it is kept
Data is kept only while the account is connected. Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens.

## Deleting your data
To have your data deleted, remove the app in your Facebook settings (**Settings & privacy → Settings → Business
integrations → Remove**), or open a request at
https://github.com/devsitaram/ClippingVideo/issues. All data linked to your account is deleted within 30 days.
